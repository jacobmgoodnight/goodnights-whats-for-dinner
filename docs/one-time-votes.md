# A vote while visiting family

From the organizer Home, choose **Start a one-time vote**. Enter 2–20 restaurant names, one per line, and the number of people voting (including yourself, maximum 20). Addresses are optional in life: this flow does not ask for them. A short town or location label can distinguish two restaurants with the same name.

Favorites is the default. Open **Meal, voting method & optional title** to use consensus or ranked voting, change Lunch/Dinner, or give this occasion a title. Create the poll, then Share or Copy its invitation into your family conversation. Sharing prepares a message; you send it yourself. The organizer opens the same invitation to vote.

Guests enter their own names, choose their ballot, and submit once. Names appear only to people with that invitation and the organizer. No account, phone number, photo, reusable personal link or permanent family membership is created. A guest can correct their name before submitting. Two guests with the same name should add an initial. After one guest votes, **Another guest on this phone?** lets the next person join. Returning on the same phone remembers the guest for this poll only.

Results appear after the chosen number of ballots, or when the organizer closes early. A submitted guest ballot cannot be edited. An identical network retry acknowledges the same ballot without changing it. Ties retain the existing saved random choice or a reviewed runoff draft with a **new invitation**. All-no consensus offers a fresh one-time shortlist. None of this records an actual meal.

The saved restaurant library and permanent member list stay as they were. Name-only choices have stable archived restaurant IDs so the poll remains readable in history; they are not active library entries. A future manual save of a matching restaurant may offer the normal archived-location review. Reusing this dinner opens a fresh one-time draft, with no previous guest identities or votes.

Share only with trusted family. Names are self-reported, not verified accounts: someone with the invitation can act under a name. Turning the invitation off in Voting options revokes it; reenabling creates a new link. A token-free results share includes no guest identities or private family credentials. The app remains one household per Supabase project.

If an unsubmitted guest loses browser storage, entering the same unused name recovers that guest. If all places are occupied, ask the organizer to help, close early, or create a fresh poll. Invitations from a deleted poll stop working. There is no automatic expiration scheduler or automatic messaging.
